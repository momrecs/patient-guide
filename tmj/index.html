Your six-week plan

🦴 Treat it like a sprained ankle

An irritated jaw joint needs rest and time — and it can't heal if you keep "walking on it."

1

Soft diet for six weeks. Nothing that takes more than about three chews — cut food small. Soft ideas below.

2

Anti-inflammatory for the first week, as directed at your visit.

3

Rest the joint: no wide yawns (tongue on the roof of your mouth helps), big sandwiches, whole apples, gum, nail-biting, chin-leaning, or phone-cradling. Sleep on your back if you can, with good neck support.

4

"Lips together, teeth apart." Most clenching happens without noticing — driving, working out, concentrating. Seal your lips and puff a little air; the jaw relaxes automatically. Repeat it whenever you catch your teeth touching.

Feeling better at week three doesn't mean healed. See the full six weeks through, or the joint can be re-injured and take much longer to settle. This is the easiest of all the treatments — if you cheat here, it only gets tougher.
Ice for new, sharp pain (first 72 hours). Moist heat for tense, tired muscles — 10 minutes, then lightly brush the area with an ice cube, rewarm, and repeat 4–5 times.
Always mention your jaw joint to your dentist or doctor before other dental or medical treatment.

Soft-food ideas

🍳

Breakfast

Oatmeal or cream of wheat, scrambled eggs, yogurt, applesauce, soft or stewed fruit, smoothies, cottage cheese.

🍝

Lunch & dinner

Pasta, mac and cheese, soups, mashed potatoes, rice and beans, crustless bread, eggs, tofu, fish, lasagna, chili, finely cut chicken or ground beef, soft-cooked veggies.

🍨

Dessert

Ice cream, pudding, Jell-O, anything blended.

Skip these for now

Gum
Bagels & hard bread
Steak & chewy meat
Whole apples & hard fruit
Raw carrots & crisp veggies
Ice cubes
Nuts, popcorn & chips
Chewy candy
Croutons & hard crackers
